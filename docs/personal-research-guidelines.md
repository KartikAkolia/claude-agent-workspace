# Personal Research Guidelines

Standing guidelines for how research should be conducted and sourced: methodology, source selection, quality screening, and output expectations.

## Research Methodology

- Find relevant or similar content related to the associated body of knowledge or subject matter.
- Correlate supporting information into the primary subject under investigation.
- Cross-reference findings across multiple authoritative sources where possible.

## Source Selection Criteria

Only use established, trusted, and authoritative sources. Examples of acceptable sources include:

- <https://monday.com/blog/project-management/swot-analysis-project-management/>
- <https://project-management.com/swot-analysis/>
- <https://www.teamgantt.com/blog/swot-analysis>

### Preferred Sources

- Official organizations, standards bodies, and regulatory authorities.
- Academic institutions and peer-reviewed publications.
- Established industry publications with editorial oversight.
- Recognized subject matter experts with verifiable credentials.
- Reputable vendors and professional organizations.

## Content Quality Assessment

### Exclude Content That Appears AI-Generated

Flag and exclude content exhibiting common indicators of low-quality AI generation, including:

- Lack of cited sources or references.
- Generic statements without supporting evidence.
- Repetitive phrasing or excessive use of filler content.
- Unverifiable claims.
- No identifiable author, editor, or publication standards.
- Poor factual consistency or unsupported conclusions.

### Verification Requirements

Before using any source:

- Verify the author or publishing organization.
- Confirm the presence of citations or supporting evidence.
- Cross-check key claims against independent authoritative sources.
- Assess whether the content demonstrates original analysis rather than repackaged or duplicated material.
- Ensure the source maintains acceptable editorial or peer-review standards.

## Plagiarism and Attribution Standards

- Do not use plagiarized content.
- Ensure all significant claims can be traced to authoritative sources.
- Prefer original sources over secondary summaries whenever available.
- Maintain proper attribution throughout the research process.

## Research Output Expectations

Research findings should:

- Be evidence-based.
- Reference authoritative sources.
- Distinguish clearly between facts, analysis, and opinion.
- Document source provenance.
- Highlight any uncertainty, limitations, or conflicting viewpoints.
